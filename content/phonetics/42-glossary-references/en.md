# Glossary and references

## English-Vietnamese glossary

| Term | Vietnamese | Short definition |
|---|---|---|
| acoustic phonetics | âm học | the branch studying the acoustic signal of speech |
| allophone | biến thể âm vị | a concrete realisation of a phoneme |
| antiformant | phản cộng hưởng | a dip in the spectrum caused by a side cavity (such as the nasal cavity) |
| articulation | cấu âm | producing sounds with the speech organs |
| aspiration | bật hơi | a voiceless breathy interval after a stop release |
| assimilation | đồng hoá | a sound becoming more like a neighbour |
| auditory phonetics | thính học (ngữ âm) | the branch studying hearing and perception of sounds |
| broad transcription | phiên âm rộng | phonemic transcription, with little detail |
| cardinal vowels | nguyên âm chuẩn | Daniel Jones' reference vowel system |
| coarticulation | đồng cấu âm | overlap of articulatory movements of neighbouring sounds |
| coda | phần cuối âm tiết | the consonants after the nucleus |
| coalescence | hoà âm | two sounds merging into one (did you → dʒ) |
| complementary distribution | phân bố bổ sung | allophones occurring in mutually exclusive environments |
| diacritic | dấu phụ | a mark added to an IPA symbol to add detail |
| diphthong | nguyên âm đôi | a vowel gliding from one position to another within a syllable |
| distinctive feature | đặc trưng khu biệt | the smallest property distinguishing sounds |
| elision | lược âm | dropping of a sound in connected speech |
| F0 (fundamental frequency) | tần số cơ bản | the vibration rate of the vocal folds, perceived as pitch |
| formant | formant | a spectral peak caused by a resonance of the vocal tract |
| fricative | âm xát | a consonant with friction through a narrow gap |
| functional load | tải chức năng | the number of word pairs a contrast distinguishes |
| glottis | thanh môn | the gap between the vocal folds |
| harmonic | họa âm | an integer multiple of the fundamental frequency |
| IPA | bảng phiên âm quốc tế | the International Phonetic Alphabet |
| intonation | ngữ điệu | pitch variation over a phrase or utterance |
| lexical set | bộ từ khoá | a class of words sharing a vowel, named by a keyword (Wells) |
| manner of articulation | phương thức cấu âm | the kind of obstruction of the airflow |
| minimal pair | cặp tối thiểu | two words differing in just one sound |
| narrowband spectrogram | phổ đồ hẹp | long window, shows harmonics |
| neutralisation | trung hoà | loss of a contrast in a position |
| nucleus | hạt nhân (âm tiết / ngữ điệu) | the core of a syllable; the syllable with the main pitch movement |
| onset | phần đầu âm tiết | the consonants before the nucleus |
| phoneme | âm vị | the smallest sound unit that changes word meaning |
| phonation | phát thanh | how the vocal folds behave |
| phonotactics | quy tắc phối hợp âm | the sound sequences allowed in a language |
| place of articulation | vị trí cấu âm | where a moving articulator approaches a fixed one |
| plosive | âm tắc | a consonant with complete closure then release |
| pitch accent | trọng âm cao độ | labels such as H*, L* in ToBI on stressed syllables |
| sonority | độ vang | how "vowel-like" a sound is |
| source-filter model | mô hình nguồn - bộ lọc | speech = a sound source + a resonant filter |
| spectrogram | phổ đồ | a plot of energy against time and frequency |
| stress | trọng âm | the prominence of a syllable |
| suprasegmental | siêu đoạn | stress, rhythm, intonation, tone |
| tone | thanh điệu | pitch on a syllable that distinguishes words |
| transcription | phiên âm | writing sounds with symbols |
| velum | vòm mềm | the soft part of the palate, opening and closing the nasal route |
| voice onset time (VOT) | thời gian bắt đầu thanh giọng | the interval from stop release to the onset of vocal fold vibration |
| voicing | hữu thanh | vocal fold vibration during a sound |
| wideband spectrogram | phổ đồ rộng | short window, shows formants |

## Notes on reliability

- The **male formant** data (Peterson and Barney 1952) come from a widely reproduced summary table; **Hillenbrand et al. (1995)** data are cited but **not copied**, please consult the original.
- The **F0** and **VOT** values in the lessons are **approximate illustrations**; consult the original sources (Titze 1989, Baken and Orlikoff 2000, Lisker and Abramson 1964, Cho and Ladefoged 1999) for exact figures.
- The **Vietnamese** descriptions (consonants, vowels, tones) follow Hanoi speech; other accents and other authors may differ. The Chao numerals for tones are only approximate descriptions.
- Publication details of the references are given to the compiler's knowledge and cross-checked against the source pages; **before citing in academic work, verify against the originals.**
- This content is an **introductory course**, not a substitute for the original textbooks above.
