# Diacritics and secondary articulation

## Diacritics

The basic IPA symbols record sounds only at a "broad" level. To record more **detail** we add **diacritics** to a symbol. Some important ones:

| Mark | Name | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ʰ` | aspiration | `[pʰ]` | extra voiceless time after the release |
| `̥` | devoiced | `[n̥]` | normally voiced but voiceless here |
| `̬` | voiced | `[s̬]` | normally voiceless but vibrating here |
| `̩` | syllabic | `[n̩]` | consonant forming the syllable nucleus |
| `̃` | nasalised | `[ã]` | nasalised vowel |
| `ː` | long | `[iː]` | long vowel or consonant |
| `̪` | dental | `[t̪]` | placed at the teeth instead of the ridge |
| `ʷ` | labialised | `[kʷ]` | secondary articulation: rounded lips |

Marks can sit above, below or to the right of a symbol.

## Secondary articulation

A sound can have **two simultaneous articulations**: a **primary** one (defining place and manner) and a lighter **secondary** one.

| Secondary articulation | Symbol | Part involved | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Labialisation** | `ʷ` | rounded lips | `[kʷ]` |
| **Palatalisation** | `ʲ` | tongue body raised towards the hard palate | `[tʲ]` (Russian) |
| **Velarisation** | `ˠ` or `ɫ` | tongue body retracted towards the soft palate | `[ɫ]` "dark l" |
| **Pharyngealisation** | `ˤ` | tongue root retracted towards the pharynx | `[sˤ]` (Arabic) |

English has a very familiar example: /l/ has two forms: the **"clear l"** `[l]` (before a vowel, as in *leaf*) and the **"dark l"** `[ɫ]` (after a vowel or at the end of a word, as in *full*). The dark form has a velarised/pharyngealised secondary articulation. General American uses the "dark l" in most positions.

## Syllabic consonants

Normally a vowel is the syllable nucleus. But highly sonorous consonants (`/n/`, `/l/`) can also be the nucleus, as in *button* `[ˈbʌʔn̩]`, *bottle* `[ˈbɒtɫ̩]`. The small mark below shows this.

## Examples of narrow transcription

| Word | Broad | Narrow (common British) |
|---|---|---|
| pin | `/pɪn/` | `[pʰɪn]` |
| spin | `/spɪn/` | `[spɪn]` (unaspirated) |
| full | `/fʊl/` | `[fʊɫ]` |
| button | `/ˈbʌtn/` | `[ˈbʌʔn̩]` |

Narrow transcription shows the **variants** that broad transcription ignores, and opens the way to phonology in module 6.
