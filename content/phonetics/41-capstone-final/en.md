# Review: Final review

A gentle review with **no score**. If you get one wrong, read the answer and explanation and try again.

## A small task

**Capstone project** (do it yourself, ungraded): choose **one** of the two.

1. **Analyse your own voice.** Record yourself reading three English sentences. Transcribe the hard words (broad), mark the stress, measure the F0 of one question and one statement, and measure F1-F2 of two vowels. Write half a page naming **one** thing you want to improve and why.
2. **A small study.** Follow the study design lesson, for example measure the VOT of /t/ and /d/ for at least 3 people. Write a short report with the question, how you measured, results (with units) and limitations.
