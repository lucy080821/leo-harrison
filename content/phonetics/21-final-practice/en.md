# Final review

Congratulations on finishing the course! Here are a few questions to look back, **with no score**. If you get one wrong, look at the answer; it's fine.

## One thing to try yourself (not graded)

Choose a short passage of three or four English sentences that you like. Then do these in turn:

1. **Transcribe** the words you find hard, using a dictionary to check.
2. **Mark the stress** on multi-syllable words and underline the **content words**.
3. **Record** yourself reading it, listen back and find **one** thing to improve (a sound, a stress, or a bit of intonation).
4. Read again and record once more. Listen for what changed.

You don't need to sound "perfect" straight away. Simply **hearing the difference** is a real step forward.
