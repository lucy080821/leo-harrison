# Review: Articulatory phonetics

A gentle review with **no score**. If you get one wrong, read the answer and explanation and try again.

## A small task

Say *two* and *tea* in front of a mirror and watch the shape of your lips **already at the /t/**. You will see anticipatory coarticulation with your own eyes.
