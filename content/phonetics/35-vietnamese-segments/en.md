# Vietnamese phonetics: consonants, vowels and the syllable

To contrast with English we need to understand the Vietnamese sound system. This lesson describes **Hanoi Vietnamese** following **Kirby (2011)** in the *Illustrations of the IPA* series of the *Journal of the International Phonetic Association*, cross-checked against general descriptions. Other regional accents (Hue, Saigon) differ, and different authors may analyse slightly differently.

## Overview

- About **19 core consonants**, **9 monophthong qualities** plus short vowels and **3 diphthongs**, and **6 tones**.
- The syllable has the structure **(C₁)(w)V(G|C₂)+T**: an optional initial consonant, an optional medial /w/, a vowel, an optional final and a **tone**.
- **No consonant clusters**; each syllable is usually a morpheme.

Hanoi lacks the retroflex sounds `ʂ`, `ʐ`, `ʈ` (found in some other accents). The sound /w/ is the only consonant that can form a cluster with another (as in *qu*, *hoa*, *toàn*).

## Vowels (Hanoi)

**Monophthongs** (9 qualities):

| | Front | Central/back unrounded | Back rounded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Close | `i` (i, y) | `ɯ` (ư) | `u` (u) |
| Close-mid | `e` (ê) | `ɤ` (ơ) | `o` (ô) |
| Open-mid | `ɛ` (e) | | `ɔ` (o) |
| Open | `a` (a) | | |

There are also **short** vowels `ă` and `â` (`ɤ̆`), appearing only in **closed syllables**.

**Centring diphthongs**: `iə` (ia, iê), `ɯə` (ưa, ươ), `uə` (ua, uô).

An alternative analysis counts **11 monophthongs** and **3 diphthongs**, depending on how the short vowels are treated.

Apart from `u o ɔ` and the diphthong `uə`, the vowels are **unrounded**. In southern accents high vowels may be diphthongised in open syllables.

## Finals and glides

- **Final consonants**: `p`, `t`, `k`, `m`, `n`, `ŋ`. The three stops `p t k` are **unreleased** and occur only with the **sắc** or **nặng** tones.
- **Final glides**: `j` (i, y) and `w` (u, o), forming rhymes such as *ai, ao, ui, eo*. They do **not** combine with a final consonant.

## Differences from English: points to remember

| Aspect | Vietnamese | English |
|---|---|---|
| Consonant clusters | none | up to 3 initial, 4 final |
| Final consonants | about 6 (+ glides) | most consonants |
| `/θ ð/` | absent | present |
| `/tʰ/` and `/t/` | **contrast** | one phoneme |
| Tones | 6 | none |
| Airstream | pulmonic (and `ɓ ɗ`) | pulmonic |
| Vowels | 9 monophthong qualities + short + 3 diphthongs | 12 monophthongs + 8 diphthongs (RP) |

Some of these differences (clusters, final consonants, tones) strongly affect Vietnamese speakers learning English pronunciation. The next two lessons examine them in more depth.
